How It Works & Formula

Percentage means "per one hundred". You can solve any percentage question with simple math:

  • Basic Percentage: Formula = (X / 100) × Y
  • Find Percentage Share: Formula = (Part / Total) × 100
  • % Difference / Change: Formula = ((Final - Initial) / Initial) × 100

Real Life Examples

Shopping Discount (20% Off)

A shirt costs $80. What is 20% off? 20% of 80 = $16 discount. You pay $64.

Exam Marks Percentage

Scored 480 out of 600? (480 ÷ 600) × 100 = 80% marks.

Salary or Price Hike

Price went from $200 to $250? (50 ÷ 200) × 100 = 25% increase.
